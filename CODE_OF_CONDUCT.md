## Code of Conduct

## Our Community

`Prism2D.js` is a community for developers, game creators, testers, and anyone interested in building and improving the engine.

We want everyone to feel welcome, respected, and comfortable taking part in the project. Whether you are contributing code, asking questions, sharing a project, or simply learning, your participation matters.

By participating in the `Prism2D.js` community, you agree to follow these guidelines and help create a positive environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect, kindness, and professionalism.
- Be open to different opinions, experiences, and ideas.
- Give constructive feedback, especially when discussing code or technical decisions.
- Help maintain a friendly, inclusive, and welcoming environment.
- Respect the privacy and personal boundaries of other community members.
- Use English in official Prism2D.js community channels whenever possible, so more people can follow and participate.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not allowed:

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults, or intimidation.
- Discrimination or hateful behavior.
- Sharing private or personal information without permission.
- Spam, flooding, or excessive self-promotion.
- Threats, encouragement of violence, or illegal activities.
- Explicit sexual or NSFW content.
- Religious discussions or debates.
- Repeatedly promoting unrelated projects or content.

Political discussions are allowed as long as they remain respectful and do not turn into harassment, personal attacks, or spam.

## Projects and Promotion

Games and projects created with Prism2D.js are welcome in the community. We are happy to see what you are building and to celebrate your progress.

Other projects may also be shared when they are relevant to the community. However, repeated advertising, excessive promotion, or using the community primarily to promote unrelated projects is not allowed.

## Technical Discussions

Technical disagreements are normal and welcome. Different perspectives often help the project improve.

Members may criticize code, suggest different approaches, or disagree with technical decisions. However, criticism should remain focused on the code, design, or idea rather than the person. Try to explain your reasoning clearly and keep discussions respectful, even when opinions differ.

## Moderation

Moderators help keep the community organized, welcoming, and safe for everyone.

Depending on the situation, moderators may:

- Ask members to stop inappropriate behavior.
- Remove messages or content that violate the rules.
- Warn members about their behavior.
- Restrict participation when necessary.
- Recommend further action for serious or repeated violations.

Administrators may take stronger action when necessary, including permanent bans in serious cases.

Moderation decisions should consider the circumstances and severity of the situation. Not every violation requires the same response, and moderators will try to handle each situation fairly and thoughtfully.

## Project Authority

The Prism2D.js project owner has final authority over project governance and major community decisions.

This does not prevent moderators, administrators, or contributors from sharing their opinions, raising concerns, or making recommendations. Their input is encouraged and will be considered when making important decisions.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the project moderation team through the appropriate official Prism2D.js community channel.

Please provide enough information for the situation to be understood and reviewed. If possible, include relevant messages, links, dates, or other context.

Reports should be handled respectfully and, when appropriate, kept private. Thank you for helping us address problems and keep the community welcoming.

## Enforcement

We expect everyone participating in Prism2D.js to help maintain a respectful and constructive community.

Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from community spaces or other appropriate action.

Thank you to everyone who helps make Prism2D.js a welcoming place to build, learn, test, share ideas, and contribute.
